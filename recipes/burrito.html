Burrito

Ingredients:

  • 2 large flour tortillas
  • 200g cooked chicken breast, shredded
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 cup black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 cup corn kernels
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Monterey Jack)
  • 1/4 cup salsa
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large bowl, combine the shredded chicken, cooked rice, black beans, corn, ground cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Mix well to combine all the ingredients.
  2. Lay out the flour tortillas on a clean surface. Divide the chicken and rice mixture evenly between the two tortillas, placing it in the center of each tortilla.
  3. Sprinkle the shredded cheese over the chicken and rice mixture. Add a spoonful of salsa and a dollop of sour cream on top. Sprinkle with chopped fresh cilantro.
  4. Fold in the sides of the tortilla and then roll it up tightly from the bottom to the top, creating a burrito. Repeat with the second tortilla.
  5. Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Place the burritos seam-side down in the skillet and cook for 2-3 minutes on each side, or until the tortillas are golden brown and the cheese has melted.
  6. Remove the burritos from the skillet and let them cool for a minute before slicing in half. Serve with extra salsa, sour cream, or guacamole on the side. Enjoy your delicious homemade burrito!

Tips:

  • You can customize your burrito by adding other ingredients like sautéed bell peppers, onions, or avocado slices.
  • For a vegetarian option, you can replace the chicken with grilled vegetables or tofu.
  • If you prefer a spicier burrito, you can add some chopped jalapeños or a dash of hot sauce to the filling.
